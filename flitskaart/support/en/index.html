Flitskaart support

Flitskaart is a flashcard app for iPhone and iPad, for vocabulary, geography and concepts. It reschedules every card for the moment you are about to forget it. More at apps.jal.ink/flitskaart.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn a word list into cards?
Open a deck, choose Import cards and take or choose a photo. Review the recognized cards and tap Use. You can also paste a list, one word and its translation per line, separated by a tab or semicolon.

What is free and what comes with Flitskaart Full?
For free you can create 3 decks of your own; subdecks, folders and the sample decks don't count, and studying is always complete. The one-time purchase Flitskaart Full adds unlimited decks and sync between your devices. With Family Sharing, one purchase works for the whole family.

I already bought it, but the app isn't unlocked.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with, or make sure Family Sharing is on.

How do I sync between iPhone and iPad?
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both devices, signed in with the same Apple Account. This needs iOS 17 or later and is part of Flitskaart Full.

How do I practise both directions?
Open Edit deck and set Study direction to Both. You first practise one way and then the other, each with its own schedule.

How do I share a deck?
Touch and hold the deck and choose Share. The recipient opens the file in Flitskaart and adds it as a new deck, without your progress.

The voice sounds robotic.
Download an enhanced voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, then pick it in Flitskaart under Settings › Voice for [language].
