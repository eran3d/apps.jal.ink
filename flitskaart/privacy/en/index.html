Flitskaart privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Flitskaart follows the general privacy policy, with the additions below. This policy covers Flitskaart on iPhone and iPad and on Android.

In short

Flitskaart collects no personal data. There is no account, no server of its own, no ads and no tracking. The developer sees nothing of what you do in the app. On Android, Google's text recognition (ML Kit) sends anonymous data about the device and how it performs to Google; see below. That applies to everyone, including children.

What the app stores

Your decks, cards, progress, streaks and source photos are stored on your device.

On iPhone and iPad: if you turn on iCloud sync, they are also kept in your own private iCloud so they appear on your other iPhones and iPads. That storage is covered by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

On Android: if device backup is on, Android includes the app's data in the encrypted backup in your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. That backup is covered by Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it. A JSON backup you export yourself is stored wherever you keep it.

Photos and speech

When you photograph a word list, the text is recognized on your device. On iPhone and iPad this uses Apple Vision, optionally with help from Apple Intelligence running on the device itself. On Android it uses Google's ML Kit, also on the device. In both cases the photo is not sent to a server. Read-aloud uses the voices on your device.

ML Kit diagnostics (Android)

Text recognition and language identification on Android come from Google ML Kit. ML Kit sends Google data to monitor and improve how it works: your device's manufacturer, model and Android version, information about the app and how fast recognition runs, error codes, a per-installation number that is not meant to identify you or your device, and for language identification which languages were detected. The recognized text and your photos are not part of this. Google does not share this data with third parties and sends it encrypted. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders, card notifications and widgets are scheduled and filled on your device. Nothing goes through a server.

Purchases

The one-time purchase Flitskaart Full is handled by Apple or Google Play. The app only learns that the purchase succeeded; payment details stay with Apple or Google.

What you send yourself

When you share a deck, or send feedback or a poorly recognized photo, that only happens when you choose to, through the share menu (such as AirDrop, Messages or WhatsApp) or your own mail app. A feedback mail includes the app and operating system version and the device type so a problem can be traced. Such a mail is only used to answer your question and improve the app.

Deleting data

Delete decks and cards in the app. Delete the app to erase all local data. Remove data from iCloud in the app before deleting it, or via Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Manage an Android backup in your Google account through your device settings or at drive.google.com.
