Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy applies to all apps by Eran Jalink. Anything specific to an app is listed on that app's privacy page.

In short

  • No account needed, unless the app says otherwise.
  • No analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your data stays on your device or in your own iCloud. The developer has no access to it.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

iCloud

When an app uses iCloud, or its data is included in your device's iCloud backup, that storage is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting data

Delete data within the app, or delete the app to remove all local data.

Children

The apps collect no personal data from anyone, including children.
