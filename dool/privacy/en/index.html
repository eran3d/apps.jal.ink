Dool privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Dool follows the general privacy policy, with the additions below.

In short

Dool collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your goals, activities and filled-in days are stored only on your device. They are included in your device's regular iCloud backup if you have it turned on; that backup is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to this data.

Network

Dool does not connect to the internet or to any server. The animations are bundled in the app itself. When you share a dool with someone nearby, its data travels directly from device to device over the local network, without a server in between.

Deleting data

Delete a goal in the app's settings, or delete the app to remove all your data.
