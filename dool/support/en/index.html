Dool support

Dool is a calm habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. Each goal is a grid of days; fill in a day when you did what you set out to do, and see at a glance whether you are on track.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your own device. It is included in your regular iCloud backup, so it comes back when you set up a new device from that backup.

How do I delete a goal?
Open the goal, tap the button with the three dots and choose "Delete Dool" at the bottom. This removes the goal and all filled-in days.

How do I clear a filled-in day?
Tap the day and tap the activity again.
